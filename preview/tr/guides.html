To open Settings, start PoDee again from the Start menu. No second island appears; the running app opens its settings.

Claude Code on the island

The island shows what the agent is busy with and asks you when it needs permission.

  1. Open Agents

    Settings → Agents → Claude Code.

  2. Connect an account

    Each account folder has its own row and its own Connect button. With several accounts, press Connect all. You can disconnect them one by one.

  3. Restart your sessions

    PoDee adds hooks to settings.json and keeps a copy as settings.json.podee-backup. Claude Code sessions that are already open pick them up after a restart.

  4. Watch it by the clock

    Appearance → “Next to the clock”: Claude's work shows right on the closed island.

Google or Outlook calendar

PoDee reads your calendar from an iCal link. No login or password needed.

  1. Add a calendar

    Settings → Calendar → “Connect calendar”. Enter a name and the iCal link.

  2. The link from Google

    In Google Calendar: Settings → your calendar → “Integrate calendar” → “Secret address in iCal format”. It ends in basic.ics. The public address only works if the calendar is public.

  3. The link from Outlook

    In Outlook: Settings → Calendar → Shared calendars → “Publish a calendar”. Pick the calendar and “Can view all details”, press Publish and copy the ICS link.

  4. Reminders

    Events refresh every 15 minutes. Choose how early to be reminded of a meeting in “Remind me”.

Voice and AI chat

Voice commands, dictation and questions to AI, right on the island.

  1. Choose recognition

    Settings → Voice → Recognition. Whisper on your PC works offline: the base model is 150 MB, small 490 MB (our pick), medium 1.5 GB. There's also cloud Whisper with a free Groq key, and Windows speech recognition.

  2. Speak

    Hold Ctrl+Alt+Space and say a command, such as “open Telegram”. Ctrl+Alt+D starts dictation.

  3. Connect AI chat

    Settings → AI chat → Provider. With an API key: “Get a key” opens the provider's page, and Gemini's key is free. Without a key, chat runs through a subscription: Claude Code, Gemini CLI or Codex.

  4. Where the keys live

    In Windows Credential Manager, not in the settings file.

PoDoo + PoDee

A phone with PoDoo controls the island: opens it, starts a timer, adds tasks and asks PoDy.

  1. Install PoDoo

    PoDoo goes on your PC and on your phone. How to install

  2. Turn on the link

    In PoDee: Settings → Modules → “PoDoo link”. Commands are taken only from PoDoo on the same PC.

  3. Control it from your phone

    Open the PoDee page in PoDoo: the island opens and hides, timers and tasks take one tap. PoDy's answer arrives right on the phone.
