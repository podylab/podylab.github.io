Good to know

Do I need an account or the internet?

No account. The phone and the PC only need to be on one local network or linked by a cable or Bluetooth. PoDoo itself works without the internet.

Does it work without the PC program?

No. The app on the phone is only the screen; the program on the PC runs the actions. That's why the PC program is required.

Which phones will do?

Any phone or tablet with Android 5.0 or newer. An old phone from a drawer works too, and gets a new job. PoDoo has no iPhone app yet.

What does the app see?

Only the network and the camera, and the camera is used just to scan the QR code. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

Can I connect without Wi-Fi?

Yes. Plug the phone into the PC with a USB cable or pair them over Bluetooth, and pick that way on the app's first screen.
