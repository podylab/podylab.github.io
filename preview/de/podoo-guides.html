Connecting the phone

PoDoo waits in the tray on the PC. The phone finds it by itself on the same network.

  1. Over Wi-Fi

    Open the app on the phone. The PC with PoDoo shows up in the list: tap it, check that the code matches on both screens, and allow the connection on the PC.

  2. If the PC is not found

    Tap “Can’t find your PC?”. Scan the QR code in the PoDoo window, type the six digits from the PC screen, or enter the address by hand.

  3. Over a USB cable

    Plug in the cable and turn on “USB tethering” on the phone. Android turns it off every time the cable is unplugged, so turn it on each time. The app then finds the PC by itself.

  4. Over Bluetooth

    First pair the phone with the PC in Windows settings. Then pick Bluetooth on the first screen of the app. The first load takes a few seconds.

Buttons and pages

Everything is set up on the PC with the mouse. The phone updates right away.

  1. Add a button

    A button can be an app, a website, a folder or file, a key combination, a script or a set of actions. Apps can simply be dragged onto the page.

  2. Build a set of actions

    Several steps in one button: open a project, start music, mute notifications.

  3. Tie a page to an app

    Pick apps for a page. When one of them is active on the PC, the dock switches to that page by itself. With none picked, you swipe to it.

  4. Pick a theme

    Sketchbook, Clay, Console, Poster, Glass and more. You can set your own background.

PoDoo + PoDee

With PoDee on the PC, PoDoo gets a PoDee page. From it you control the PoDee island right from your phone.

  1. Install PoDee

    You need PoDee 1.3.5 and PoDoo 1.0.1 or newer. Get PoDee

  2. Turn on the link in PoDee

    Settings → Modules → “Link with PoDoo”. Nothing to turn on in PoDoo itself.

  3. Open the PoDee page

    It shows up in the dock on the phone by itself. Open or hide the island, start a timer, add a task, ask PoDy. The answer from PoDy comes to the phone, and the chat is kept.

PoDee takes commands only from PoDoo on the same PC.

Questions

Brauche ich ein Konto oder Internet?

Kein Konto. Handy und PC müssen nur im selben lokalen Netz sein oder per Kabel oder Bluetooth verbunden. PoDoo läuft ohne Internet.

Geht es ohne das PC-Programm?

Nein. Die Handy-App ist nur der Bildschirm, die Aktionen macht das PC-Programm. Deshalb braucht es das PC-Programm.

Welche Handys passen?

Jedes Handy oder Tablet mit Android 5.0 oder neuer. Auch ein altes Handy aus der Schublade passt und bekommt so einen neuen Job. Für das iPhone gibt es PoDoo noch nicht.

Was sieht die App?

Nur das Netz und die Kamera, und die Kamera nur zum Scannen des QR-Codes. Nichts geht an uns oder an sonst jemanden.

Geht es ohne WLAN?

Ja. Schließ das Handy per USB-Kabel an den PC an oder kopple beide per Bluetooth, und wähle das auf dem ersten Bildschirm der App.
