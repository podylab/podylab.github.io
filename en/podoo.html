A remote for your PC from your phone

Stand a phone or tablet by your monitor and it shows the icons of your apps. One tap and Photoshop, Telegram or your project folder is open. No need to buy a Stream Deck. Any Android phone works, and an old one from your drawer gets a second life.

What PoDoo does

An Android phone and a Windows PC, connected over Wi-Fi, cable or Bluetooth.

Quick launch

Icons of your favourite apps, sites and folders are already there. Nothing to set up.

Buttons for each app

Photoshop is open, the phone shows its tools. OBS is open, it shows scenes, the mic and going live.

Music and touchpad

Pause, volume, next track. Scroll through a film on the TV from the sofa.

Getting started

1. Install on your PC

Download PoDoo for Windows and run the setup. It sits in the tray and waits for your phone.

2. Install on your phone

Download PoDoo.apk on the phone itself and open it. Android asks to allow installs from your browser: allow it.

3. Connect

With the phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi, the app finds your computer. Tap it and enter the code shown in PoDoo.
