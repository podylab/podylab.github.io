Fixes and what's new

What changed in each version of PoDee. Updates arrive on their own.

1.9.4

Latest2026-11-12
  • In AnalogOS the battery now sits on the menu bar next to the date, with its percent. Wi-Fi, sound and the clock stay on the taskbar.
  • The widgets' edit bar, their settings and the list are light in the light theme and dark in the dark one, every label readable.
  • Open widget settings switch to the widget you click.
  • The «Soft square» shape no longer turns a long widget into an oval, and «Circle» no longer cuts off text.
  • Fixed: a right click on the Bitrix24 tray icon didn't open its window, and some apps' tray icons vanished after PoDee restarted.

1.9.3

2026-11-11
  • The AnalogOS transparency slider now takes effect in every panel at once. Before, the taskbar could stay see-through at 0% until a restart.
  • At 0%, liquid glass and widgets are solid too.

1.9.2

2026-11-11
  • AnalogOS: choose your glass, frosted or liquid. What lies behind the menu bar, the taskbar and the section windows shows through, app windows included.
  • One transparency slider for both: at 0% the panels are solid, at 100% there is no haze.
  • All of AnalogOS is glass now: Start, menus, quick settings and a new power screen. Settings sit on Windows' own glass.
  • New AnalogOS wallpapers, Dunes and Fan, and a side taskbar with a blue Start.
  • Close a notification on the island with ✕ or a swipe; taskbar icons get an unread dot.
  • Speech recognition knows names, and the new large-v3-turbo model hears them best.
  • Fixed: the Microsoft Store version set a black wallpaper.
  • Fixed: a blurred strip stayed over a full-screen game or remote desktop.

1.9.1

2026-11-10
  • PoDee is lighter: at rest it uses about half the processor time and around a third less memory.
  • Modules that are off no longer get windows, so a disabled taskbar or widgets layer takes no memory.
  • The AnalogOS menu bar no longer slips down after sleep or an Explorer restart.

1.9.0

2026-11-09
  • New AnalogOS theme, light and dark. A full-width menu bar on top, the sections' icons on it, each with its own window. A liquid glass taskbar below: big app icons, a bin drawn as a bucket, the clock and status icons right on the glass. The Inter font and four wallpapers: Hills, Folds, Layers and Wave.
  • The assistant writes in messengers. Say «text Pete on Telegram: I'll be there at seven»: it finds the chat, types the message and sends it once you press Send. «Saved Messages» and «myself» go to Telegram. It no longer opens a Windows program in place of a messenger.
  • Windows wallpapers can be picked right in the gallery, and transparency goes up to 100%.
  • Right-click on the island opens PoDee's menu: Settings and Thanks, without Refresh and Save as.
  • A Claude Code agent's question leaves the island once you answer it in the terminal.
  • Liquid glass doesn't blink when the wallpaper changes and stays on while things move.
  • The «^» list and quick settings on the taskbar no longer jump or shrink while open.
  • Less memory: speech recognition models load only while recording.
  • Drag a file onto an app's icon on the taskbar: a ring lights round the icon and the app's window comes up so you can drop the file into it. Over the clock and the system icons the cursor shows you can't drop there, as in Windows.
  • The bin on the taskbar lights up while a file is held over it, and a full bin shows papers inside.
  • After start the taskbar shows up sooner and with its icons at once, with no empty strip at the bottom of the screen.
  • In AnalogOS the indicator's settings no longer offer what doesn't work on the menu bar.

1.8.2

2026-11-08
  • New widget «Player with lyrics»: the cover, time and length, seek on the bar, the buttons and the song's words under it, shown or hidden with one button.
  • Lyrics on the desktop no longer jump: a long line fits in its place, in a slightly smaller face.
  • Taskbar on the left or right: icons at their normal size, full height too, and the bar no wider than it needs. In the Minecraft theme the clock and tiles are slots of the column, the tiles show their icons.
  • Minecraft theme: bigger words on the taskbar tiles.
  • Names on hover over the clock, tiles and Minecraft slots are easy to read: they used to be tiny.
  • Weather on the taskbar shows at once, not after five minutes; if the network isn't up yet, PoDee asks again in half a minute.
  • Text fits everywhere: a name on hover by the screen's edge no longer runs off it, the limits' reset times aren't cut, Control Center tiles and the toggles fit long names.

1.8.1

2026-11-08
  • The × on the taskbar closes a window for sure: if the app doesn't close by itself, PoDee ends it after a few seconds. A window asking about unsaved work, an app in the tray and the app's other windows are left alone.
  • Full-width taskbar: icons no longer jump when an app closes.
  • Minecraft theme: the weather, music and clock tiles on the taskbar are square in every look of the taskbar, and on the light taskbar their words are dark and easy to read.

1.8.0

2026-11-08
  • New theme «Orbit»: every word and number made of dots, its own typeface with Cyrillic, one accent colour, dark and light. The island, taskbar, widgets, Settings and the power screen in one style.
  • Power screen in «Orbit»: lock, sleep, shut down, restart or sign out with the arrows or the mouse, with how long the PC has been on.
  • Wallpapers: a gallery that shows each one big before you set it; clouds by day and night, «Eclipse», «Planet», three Minecraft pictures.
  • Taskbar «Over all windows» keeps its strip of the screen: maximized windows no longer go under it.
  • Taskbar: quick settings like Windows 11, a rail on the side edge, tray icons remembered between runs, the AnyDesk icon shows again.
  • Health: its own page, eye exercises with sounds.
  • Widget settings in tabs; options that don't fit the theme are hidden.
  • Charging is lit more calmly: red only below 20 %.

1.7.0

2026-11-06
  • Screenshot: PrtSc or a key of your own freezes every screen, then select a part, mark it up, pin it, print it or pass it on. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Kazakh: 17 languages now.
  • General: one switch shows or hides everything at once, or set the island, widgets and taskbar one by one.
  • Taskbar: the icon of an open app brings up its window instead of a new copy. Clicks work right up to the taskbar's edge.
  • VPN connects and disconnects from the taskbar with one button, with no password window.
  • Blinking tray icons no longer jump around.
  • Drag-selecting on the desktop works with hidden icons too, behind the widgets and the taskbar.
  • Less CPU when idle and less memory.

1.6.2

2026-11-05
  • Put the island on the left, in the centre, on the right or anywhere along the top edge: by the handle at the bottom of its panel, or in settings.
  • Taskbar: a new «Merge» look with curved edges, while «Classic» now simply sits against the screen's edge.
  • Taskbar with no background: every plate has the same corners.
  • Settings: the indicator's position is drawn as a simple mark.

1.6.1

2026-11-05
  • Widgets and the island on a second monitor: drag a widget past the screen's edge, move the island with a button on its panel.
  • «Song lyrics» widget: the lines follow the music, and it hides when a song has no lyrics.
  • «Move settings» page: everything you set up in one file, with no keys or passwords in it.
  • Dark glass for the island: the wallpaper shows through, and the chosen colour tints it.
  • Coloured island: letters and dates stay readable on any colour, and a light colour gets dark text.
  • A call on the island counts from its real start, and a program that merely holds the microphone no longer looks like a call.

1.6.0

2026-11-05
  • The taskbar: looks as the theme, white, «Midnight» and liquid glass, full width or fit, at the bottom or the top of the screen.
  • A click on a widget in the bar opens its panel right above it: music, the week’s weather, the month, timer, load, network, screen time, Claude limits, rates, downloads.
  • The blocks window works like the widgets one: sections, search, cards dragged onto the bar.
  • Smart rules: music pauses before a meeting, a warning about Claude’s weekly limit, quiet hours, rules per app.
  • New widgets: Gmail, Cursor, temperatures, files, stopwatch, currencies, stocks, day timeline, flip clock, YouTube, OpenAI.
  • The cat is now Barsik: its tail grows from the body and sways, faster to the music.
  • Right click on the bar and in Start as in Windows: run as administrator, Task Manager, file location, groups; Claude limits keep their shape with no numbers.
  • A «Security» page: where keys are kept, where PoDee goes online, how much memory and processor it uses.
  • Scripts can write on the island (podee.exe --say), favourite apps on Ctrl+Alt+1…6, power mode, the whole song’s text with a click on a line to seek.
  • All 16 languages are complete: errors, tips and file names now follow your language.

1.5.0

2026-11-04
  • The game strip: when a game or a film fills the screen, a slim strip takes the island's place with FPS, CPU, GPU, memory, the clock and reminders. Clicks go through it, nothing is put into the game.
  • Calls: Ctrl+Alt+M mutes every mic at once, Ctrl+Alt+K opens a camera mirror, dots on the island while the camera is on or the screen is captured.
  • Over sixty widgets: music visualizers, «Now playing» with a wave of progress, a turning record, clock faces, hourly and weekly weather, sun and moon, a focus timer, device batteries, load, Steam games, a GitHub year and more.
  • Any widget gets an «Outline» plate, a shape and a frame, and «One look for all» changes every widget at once.
  • The island's equalizer in colours, down to the cover's own; the sound is split into 32 bands.
  • Files to and from the phone with no app: the «Phone» button on the shelf shows a QR code; the phone downloads the shelf's files and sends its own photos and files to «Downloads → PoDee».
  • For streamers: OBS recording and live time and the scene on the island, a drawer with Twitch or YouTube chat or any site, a colour picker, snips and screen recording with the mic.
  • «Dock» is now «Taskbar», with a «Notch» look; the island's «Flow» shape is smooth; Settings have a «Games and calls» page, and Whisper can be removed.
  • Clearer widgets: a right click opens a widget's settings, which show only what applies to it, connected ones explain themselves with a button to their settings, photos take a file or a folder and how to fit, your own meter colours; a new «Earth: day and night» widget; soft glass tints.
  • A taskbar built from blocks: Start, the language, network, sound, battery, the clock and widget tiles (music, the next meeting, a timer, weather, load, Claude limits…) — each turns on by itself and goes left or right in any order. The widgets window is big now, with sections.

1.3.9

2026-11-03
  • The dock instead of the taskbar is closer to the real one: the input language in its corner with a list of languages, every app's name in its menu opens one more window (a second remote desktop), and the dock's Start always starts an app anew.
  • Groups on Start: drop an icon onto another's middle, open the group, rename it, drag an icon out past its edge; right click has «Make a group», «Add to the group», «Take out of the group». Start no longer slides up when many apps are pinned.
  • Quick settings: one click on the speaker mutes the sound, Wi-Fi and VPN tabs, every network around shows, the tiles switch on the first click.
  • In «^» an app opens with a click, and a double click opens the window for apps that need it (Steam).
  • Weather from two services: when one doesn't answer, it comes from the other; until it's there, the tile says so.
  • A minimized Telegram and similar apps come back with one click. Start, the bin and the clock appear smoothly when the mode changes, and Home's clock lines up with the tiles.

1.3.8

2026-11-02
  • PoDee speaks 16 languages, like this site. Pick one in Settings → General; the installer follows Windows' language too.
  • A dock instead of the taskbar: Windows' taskbar goes away and the dock gets its own Start with search, a calculator and every app, plus the Recycle Bin, the clock, the battery and quick settings (Wi-Fi, VPN, sound, Bluetooth).
  • Window previews in the dock show up at once and on every monitor; the cross on a preview closes the window. Microsoft Store apps show in the dock too.
  • The dock can sit on the left or right and hide: it slides out when the pointer touches the edge. On a second monitor with a different scale it stays put, and it comes back if Windows moves it.
  • Six new widgets: a big clock, a calendar, to-dos, a note, photos and a world clock. In the Widgets window you pick a widget by its look and drag it onto the desktop, and each can have its own text colour.
  • Holding a file over a dock icon brings that app's window forward so you can drop the file into it.
  • A timer can be cancelled from the phone through PoDoo.
  • The screen no longer flickers, PoDee doesn't steal focus from windows, Windows' taskbar hides without a flash, and the charging bolt on the dock's battery icon shows at any charge level.

  • The installer closes PoDee before updating and starts it again afterwards.
