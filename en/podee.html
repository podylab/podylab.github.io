An island at the top of your Windows screen

PoDee sits at the top edge of the screen. It shows the time and your music, listens to your voice, answers in a chat and tells you what Claude Code is up to. PoDy the cloud sits next to it and follows your mouse with its eyes.

What PoDee does

Free for everyone. Windows 10 and 11. The app speaks Russian and English.

🎵

Music

Cover, controls and lyrics in time, from any player.

🎙️

Voice and chat

Say «open Telegram» and it opens. Ask anything else of an AI: Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini.

🗓️

Calendar

Google, Outlook or any iCal link. The next meeting shows under the clock.

🤖

Claude Code

What the agent is doing in the terminal, its «allow?» questions and how much of the limit is left.

💧

Health

Water for the day, 20-20-20 eye breaks and a minute of breathing.

🧩

Widgets and dock

A dock with live window previews, and widgets on the desktop.
