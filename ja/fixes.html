Fixes and what's new

What changed in each version of PoDee. Updates arrive on their own.

1.6.0

Latest2026-11-05
  • The taskbar: looks as the theme, white, «Midnight» and liquid glass, full width or fit, at the bottom or the top of the screen.
  • A click on a widget in the bar opens its panel right above it: music, the week’s weather, the month, timer, load, network, screen time, Claude limits, rates, downloads.
  • The blocks window works like the widgets one: sections, search, cards dragged onto the bar.
  • Smart rules: music pauses before a meeting, a warning about Claude’s weekly limit, quiet hours, rules per app.
  • New widgets: Gmail, Cursor, temperatures, files, stopwatch, currencies, stocks, day timeline, flip clock, YouTube, OpenAI.
  • The cat is now Barsik: its tail grows from the body and sways, faster to the music.
  • Right click on the bar and in Start as in Windows: run as administrator, Task Manager, file location, groups; Claude limits keep their shape with no numbers.
  • A «Security» page: where keys are kept, where PoDee goes online, how much memory and processor it uses.
  • Scripts can write on the island (podee.exe --say), favourite apps on Ctrl+Alt+1…6, power mode, the whole song’s text with a click on a line to seek.
  • All 16 languages are complete: errors, tips and file names now follow your language.

1.5.0

2026-11-04
  • The game strip: when a game or a film fills the screen, a slim strip takes the island's place with FPS, CPU, GPU, memory, the clock and reminders. Clicks go through it, nothing is put into the game.
  • Calls: Ctrl+Alt+M mutes every mic at once, Ctrl+Alt+K opens a camera mirror, dots on the island while the camera is on or the screen is captured.
  • Over sixty widgets: music visualizers, «Now playing» with a wave of progress, a turning record, clock faces, hourly and weekly weather, sun and moon, a focus timer, device batteries, load, Steam games, a GitHub year and more.
  • Any widget gets an «Outline» plate, a shape and a frame, and «One look for all» changes every widget at once.
  • The island's equalizer in colours, down to the cover's own; the sound is split into 32 bands.
  • Files to and from the phone with no app: the «Phone» button on the shelf shows a QR code; the phone downloads the shelf's files and sends its own photos and files to «Downloads → PoDee».
  • For streamers: OBS recording and live time and the scene on the island, a drawer with Twitch or YouTube chat or any site, a colour picker, snips and screen recording with the mic.
  • «Dock» is now «Taskbar», with a «Notch» look; the island's «Flow» shape is smooth; Settings have a «Games and calls» page, and Whisper can be removed.
  • Clearer widgets: a right click opens a widget's settings, which show only what applies to it, connected ones explain themselves with a button to their settings, photos take a file or a folder and how to fit, your own meter colours; a new «Earth: day and night» widget; soft glass tints.
  • A taskbar built from blocks: Start, the language, network, sound, battery, the clock and widget tiles (music, the next meeting, a timer, weather, load, Claude limits…) — each turns on by itself and goes left or right in any order. The widgets window is big now, with sections.

1.3.9

2026-11-03
  • The dock instead of the taskbar is closer to the real one: the input language in its corner with a list of languages, every app's name in its menu opens one more window (a second remote desktop), and the dock's Start always starts an app anew.
  • Groups on Start: drop an icon onto another's middle, open the group, rename it, drag an icon out past its edge; right click has «Make a group», «Add to the group», «Take out of the group». Start no longer slides up when many apps are pinned.
  • Quick settings: one click on the speaker mutes the sound, Wi-Fi and VPN tabs, every network around shows, the tiles switch on the first click.
  • In «^» an app opens with a click, and a double click opens the window for apps that need it (Steam).
  • Weather from two services: when one doesn't answer, it comes from the other; until it's there, the tile says so.
  • A minimized Telegram and similar apps come back with one click. Start, the bin and the clock appear smoothly when the mode changes, and Home's clock lines up with the tiles.

1.3.8

2026-11-02
  • PoDee speaks 16 languages, like this site. Pick one in Settings → General; the installer follows Windows' language too.
  • A dock instead of the taskbar: Windows' taskbar goes away and the dock gets its own Start with search, a calculator and every app, plus the Recycle Bin, the clock, the battery and quick settings (Wi-Fi, VPN, sound, Bluetooth).
  • Window previews in the dock show up at once and on every monitor; the cross on a preview closes the window. Microsoft Store apps show in the dock too.
  • The dock can sit on the left or right and hide: it slides out when the pointer touches the edge. On a second monitor with a different scale it stays put, and it comes back if Windows moves it.
  • Six new widgets: a big clock, a calendar, to-dos, a note, photos and a world clock. In the Widgets window you pick a widget by its look and drag it onto the desktop, and each can have its own text colour.
  • Holding a file over a dock icon brings that app's window forward so you can drop the file into it.
  • A timer can be cancelled from the phone through PoDoo.
  • The screen no longer flickers, PoDee doesn't steal focus from windows, Windows' taskbar hides without a flash, and the charging bolt on the dock's battery icon shows at any charge level.

1.3.5

2026-10-31
  • PoDoo link (Settings → Modules). From your phone you can open and hide the island, start a timer, add a task and ask PoDy, and the answer comes straight to the phone.
  • The shelf converts files: images to JPG, PNG and other formats, resizing, PDF from pictures, text from images, background removal (a 170 MB model downloads on request). Video and audio through FFmpeg. Documents to PDF if Office is installed.
  • When an app in the dock has several windows, hovering shows their previews. Clicking an app that's already in front minimizes it.
  • The island no longer freezes when the Windows media service stops responding.
  • Ctrl+Alt+P tucks the island away at the top and brings it back.
  • The installer closes PoDee before updating and starts it again afterwards.
  • The tray menu has a new “Support the project” item.

1.3.4

2026-10-30
  • Whisper recognizes speech again. After the move to a new folder in 1.3.3, chat said Whisper wasn't installed. Autostart, lost in the same move, comes back on its own.
  • In the English interface, weather, hints and voice errors are in English too, and the AI answers in the language of your question. Switching language no longer resets the timer and pomodoro.
  • A version for 32-bit Windows.

1.3.3

2026-10-30
  • PoDe is now called PoDee, and the little cloud is PoDy. Settings, keys, autostart and the Claude Code connection move over on their own.
  • The heart on the island opens the Pody Lab support page, with a QR code to open it on your phone.
  • Starting PoDee a second time opens its settings instead of a second island.

1.3.1

2026-10-30
  • File Explorer and other apps started from the dock open on top of other windows, not behind them.
  • The island can hide completely, leaving only the indicator. Point at the indicator and the panel opens. Turn it on in General → When hidden.
  • The Drop, Spring, Smooth and Fast animations now feel clearly different, and the chosen one plays in Settings right away.
  • The island shapes got names: Notch, Flare, Capsule and Cloud.
  • Claude Code agents can be turned off one account at a time.
  • A crypto prices widget, and transparency for each widget and the dock.
  • Settings open without flicker, sliders reach the very edge, and the cat hangs on next to the open island too.
