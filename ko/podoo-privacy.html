PoDoo privacy policy

Effective November 1, 2026. Pody Lab, podylab@gmail.com

In short

PoDoo is an Android app and a Windows program that turn your phone into a remote for your computer. PoDoo has no servers, accounts, ads or analytics. We collect no data, and the app sends nothing to the developer or to third parties.

What stays on your devices

  • On the phone: the addresses of your computers (the last five), the chosen language, the access key to your PC and the look settings. They live in the app's storage and are removed with the app.
  • On the computer: panel settings (pages, icons, theme), the list of connected devices and the access key, in the %APPDATA%\PoDoo folder.

How the phone talks to the computer

The phone and the PC talk directly: over your home Wi-Fi network, over Bluetooth (if the phone is paired with the computer) or over a USB cable in USB tethering mode. Data never leaves these networks or Bluetooth range and never goes to the internet. So the phone can find the computer by itself, the PC program sends a short message inside the network with the computer's name and port number, without the access key. The first connection needs the QR code or the code shown on the PC; without it the computer accepts no commands.

Android permissions

  • Network access: talking to your PC over Wi-Fi.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth): only to list devices already paired with the phone and connect to your computer. The app does not scan for new devices and does not determine your location.
  • Camera: only to scan the QR code on the PC screen. The image is not saved or sent anywhere. Without the camera you can type the address.

Children

PoDoo is not meant for children under 13 and collects no data about anyone.
