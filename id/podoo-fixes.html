Fixes and what's new

What changed in each PoDoo version.

1.0.2

Latest2026-11-03
  • The timer on the PoDee page asks how long to set it for, and you can cancel it from the phone. Needs PoDee 1.3.6 or newer.
  • The PoDoo installer speaks 16 languages and follows Windows’ language; its text no longer turns into gibberish.
  • The phone app now runs on Android 7.0 or newer (it was 5.0): Google Play’s automatic protection requires it.

1.0.1

2026-10-31
  • PoDy answers right on the phone. “Ask PoDy” is now a chat: questions and answers stay after a restart. Needs PoDee 1.3.5.
  • The keyboard no longer covers the input field: asking PoDy used to blank the screen.
  • The “Glass · clouds” theme is light now: white frosted plates, dark text, mint highlight.
  • The “Support PoDoo” heart is red.

1.0.0

2026-10-31
  • First release: apps, folders and websites open from the phone in one tap. Music, volume, touchpad and keyboard too.
  • Connect over Wi-Fi (QR code or address), a USB cable or Bluetooth. The app finds the PC with PoDoo by itself.
  • Themes Sketchbook, Clay, Console, Poster, Glass and more, backgrounds and your own pictures.
  • With PoDee on the PC, a PoDee page shows up on the phone.

Every version and installer is on GitHub. Found a bug? Tell us
