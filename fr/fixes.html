Corrections et nouveautés

Ce qui a changé dans chaque version de PoDee. Les mises à jour arrivent toutes seules.

1.10.3

Dernière2026-11-28
  • Windows snapped to a side or a corner reach PoDee's taskbar, with no empty strip above it.
  • Programs that dock their own bar at a screen's edge get room for it again while PoDee runs.

1.10.2

2026-11-28
  • A program that updated itself while open has its own icon on the taskbar again, not a blank page.
  • The input language in the taskbar corner changes at once with Shift+Alt too.

1.10.1

2026-11-28
  • The interface language changes at once in every window: the island, the taskbar and the widgets.
  • A closed program’s button leaves the taskbar at once, not a few seconds later. In RetroOS its button goes and the others close up right away.
  • The input language (RU, EN…) in the taskbar corner changes at once, after Win+Space too.

1.10.0

2026-11-27
  • New RetroOS theme: a taskbar in the spirit of the early 2000s — a full-width blue bar, a green “start” with its menu, a corner with the clock and the calendar. Three color schemes (blue, green, gray) and a dark variant. The island, widgets, the settings window and the shut-down window match it too.
  • The island and the taskbar open and close more smoothly, especially on 60 Hz monitors.
  • Hotkeys: each shortcut can be changed or turned off in “Settings → Hotkeys”, and the “PoDee hotkeys” switch releases them all at once so they don’t get in the way of other programs.
  • The island and the taskbar now also step aside for players, “borderless” games and Store apps in full screen, on each monitor separately.
  • The indicator by the island looks as in Settings in every position, theme and shape. On the “Cloud” shape PoDy peeks out from behind the cloud.
  • Widgets: resize from any of the four corners; glass stays glass in any theme, and there’s a new “Like the theme” style.
  • A full-width taskbar shows “⋯” only when the icons run out of room; app names as in Windows; an app’s icon updates when it changes.
  • The taskbar can be clicked again after leaving a remote desktop and stays in place under a full-screen window.
  • Two monitors with different scaling: the taskbar and widgets are drawn at their own screen’s scale. Windows left off-screen after a monitor is unplugged are brought back.
  • Clock and menu bar: the date and time are one button, and the corner can be clicked.

1.9.12

2026-11-17
